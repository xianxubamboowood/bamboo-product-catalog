# Bamboo & wood product catalog index

Find a model in Fujian Xianxu's 2026 catalog, check its original specification, and prepare a request for quotation. This selection covers 20 cutting boards, drawer organizers, soap dishes, paper roll holders and desk organizers.

**[Search the model index on the catalog page](https://bamboowooden.com/product/2026-bamboo-wood-products-catalog-pdf/#catalog-model-index)** · **[Open the CSV index](catalog-index.csv)** · **[Get the blank quotation template](rfq/rfq-template.csv)**

## Look up a model

1. Search by model, product or material on the catalog page, or download the CSV and filter it in your spreadsheet. For example, search for `XXWSD` to find soap dishes in that family. Use the complete model number in an inquiry.
2. Open the source page link in the CSV to see the catalog photo and entry. Page numbers count from the first PDF page. Dimensions are in centimetres, in the order shown in the catalog. A range indicates a range printed for that model.
3. Copy the model and PDF page into the quotation template. Add the required quantity, destination, packaging and any changes you need. The [filled example](rfq/rfq-example.csv) illustrates a fictional buyer request. Share your completed form directly with your supplier.
